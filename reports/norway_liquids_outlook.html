Norwegian liquids supply outlook 2026–2028

On existing fields, Norwegian liquids output peaks in 2026 at 2.07 mb/d and falls to 1.78 mb/d in 2028, a loss of about 285 kb/d. Johan Sverdrup has left plateau, and the mature-field base is declining by about 10% a year. New 2027 start-ups, led by Yggdrasil and Sverdrup Phase 3, are the upside and are not in these numbers.

Johan Sverdrup

The Phase 2 plateau averaged 730 kb/d from March 2023 to September 2025. Output has fallen steadily since December 2025, from 691 to 610 kb/d in July 2026. The base case fits an Arps decline from mid-2024 at about 7% a year. Sverdrup is a third of national supply, so its decline rate is the largest single swing in the outlook: 534 to 602 kb/d in 2028 across the cases.

Mature base

83 fields, from Ekofisk and Troll to small tie-backs, produced 954 kb/d in 2025. Their combined output follows an exponential decline of about 10% a year, fitted over the last three years. Infill wells and redevelopments slow individual fields, but the group trend has been steady since 2019.

Johan Castberg

Capacity is about 205 kb/d in normal months. The FPSO had two outages in its first 13 months, so uptime matters more than reservoir decline before 2028. The base case assumes 92% uptime and a plateau to the end of 2027.

Young and redeveloped

Balder X, Breidablikk, Åsgard, Verdande, Halten Øst and seven smaller fields run at about 455 kb/d in normal months. The base case holds them flat to mid-2027, then declines them by 12% a year.

Upside not in the numbers

  • Yggdrasil (Aker BP, about 700 mmboe) is on schedule for first oil in 2027.
  • Johan Sverdrup Phase 3 (+40–50 mmboe) starts in Q4 2027 and would slow Sverdrup's decline in 2028.
  • Valhall PWP-Fenris (2027) and Skarv Satellites (August 2026).
  • Sodir reports oil output running above its own 2026 forecast, by 7.1% in May.

Risks to the view

  • Faster Sverdrup decline. June (636 kb/d) and July (610 kb/d) sit below all three cases. If they were not turnarounds, the low case is closer.
  • Castberg reliability. At 85% uptime the field loses about 15 kb/d against the base case.
  • End-of-life shut-ins in the mature tail can step output down faster than a smooth decline curve.

How far to trust it

For August 2026, the first forecast month, the base case gives 2,061 kb/d. Sodir's preliminary figure for that month is 2,073 kb/d, so the base case is 0.6% low.

The mature-field method was backtested from five starting points, June 2020 to June 2024. Its average error was 3% one year ahead and 5% two years ahead, with almost no bias in the first year. A five-year fit window overstated output at every starting point, so it is used only for the high case.
